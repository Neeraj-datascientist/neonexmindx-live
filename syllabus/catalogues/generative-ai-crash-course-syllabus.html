  • Students will learn to evaluate any new AI tool using:

  • Is it free?
  • Can it run locally?
  • What model does it use?

  • What is the license?
  • Can the tool be replaced?
  • Is it suitable for personal, academic or commercial use?
  • What are its limitations?
