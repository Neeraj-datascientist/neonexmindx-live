Direct answer

What Is the Excel, Power BI and Tableau Course?

This is one connected live online learning path that combines spreadsheet analysis, data preparation, business intelligence and dashboard storytelling.

You learn to prepare data, calculate metrics, build practical data models, create interactive reports and explain business insights using Excel, Power BI and Tableau.

Who can join?

Freshers, working professionals, MIS executives, analysts, MBA and commerce learners, managers and career switchers. Coding is not required.

What will you build?

Excel analysis models, PivotTable reports, Power BI dashboards, Tableau visualisations and an end-to-end business intelligence capstone.

Problem to solution

From Watching Tutorials to Building Practical Dashboard Proof

Employers and managers need people who can clean the data, define the KPI, build a reliable dashboard and explain the business action.

Repetitive Excel workUse structured tables, formulas, PivotTables and Power Query for repeatable workflows.
Pretty but unclear dashboardsBegin with the business question and KPI before choosing a visual.
Disconnected Power BI featuresFollow a path from connection and transformation to modelling, DAX and publishing.

Difficulty presenting insightsPractise executive summaries, recommendations and stakeholder-focused storytelling.

Complete Course Curriculum

01Excel for Analysis and Spreadsheet DesignBuild clean and reusable spreadsheet foundations.+
  • Excel interface, workbooks and worksheets
  • Productivity shortcuts
  • Data types and formatting
  • Clean table structures and spreadsheet standards
  • Raw-data, cleaned-data and output layers
  • Formula auditing and error awareness
  • Reusable workbook design

What you will build: A structured business-reporting workbook.

02Excel Formulas and Business LogicUse formulas to calculate, classify and control business information.+
  • Arithmetic calculations and reference control
  • SUM, AVERAGE, COUNT, MIN and MAX
  • Rounding functions
  • IF, AND, OR and IFERROR
  • Rule-based calculations
  • SUMIF, COUNTIF and AVERAGEIF
  • Multi-condition business logic

What you will build: A formula-driven business analysis model.

  • VLOOKUP and HLOOKUP
  • Modern lookup concepts
  • INDEX and MATCH foundations
  • LEFT, RIGHT and MID
  • TRIM, CLEAN, CONCAT and TEXTJOIN
  • Date and time functions
  • Ageing and period calculations

What you will build: A lookup and data-preparation workbook.

  • Sorting and filtering
  • Removing duplicates

  • Conditional formatting
  • Exception highlighting
  • Excel Tables and structured references
  • PivotTables and PivotCharts
  • Summary analysis

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this one combined Excel, Power BI and Tableau course?+

Yes. The course is structured as one connected learning path covering Excel analysis, Power Query, Power BI and Tableau.

Do I need coding experience?+

No. Coding is not required. The course focuses on spreadsheet analysis, data preparation, modelling, visualisation and dashboard communication.

How long is the course?+

The planned duration is approximately 7 weeks, depending on batch scheduling and project completion.

Does the course cover advanced Excel?+

Yes. It includes formulas, logical functions, lookups, text and date functions, data cleaning, PivotTables, dashboards and Power Query.

Will I learn DAX?+

Yes. You will learn DAX foundations for calculated columns, measures and practical KPI calculations.

Does the course include Power BI Service?+

Yes. Publishing, workspaces, sharing, refresh, permissions and row-level security are covered at an applied foundation level.

What will I learn in Tableau?+

You will learn data connections, charts, calculations, filters, parameters, sets, maps, actions, dashboards, story points and publishing.

Are projects included?+

Yes. You will create Excel, Power BI and Tableau projects plus an end-to-end business intelligence capstone.

Can working professionals join?+

Yes. Weekday morning, weekday evening and weekend schedules are available, subject to seat availability.

Will I receive recordings and resources?+

Yes. LMS access includes class recordings, notes and supporting learning resources.
